About

HackHeist is a developer-focused tech event by GDG on Campus MIET where students come together to learn, build, collaborate and solve real-world problems. From hands-on workshops and tech sessions to an exciting hackathon, HackHeist is all about turning ideas into working solutions while connecting with the developer community.
